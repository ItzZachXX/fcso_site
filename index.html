Serving Fayette County

The Fayette County Sheriff's Office is committed to serving and protecting the citizens of Fayette County, Kentucky, while upholding and enforcing the laws and values established by the United States Constitution.

Our goal is to maintain public safety, preserve order, and provide professional law enforcement services with integrity, respect, and dedication to the community. Every deputy comes up through our own academy and ride-along program, so the standard you see on the road is the same across the whole department.

We follow the Kentucky State Police 10-codes, phonetic alphabet, and standards, and work alongside the other agencies in Kentucky State Roleplay.

Divisions and special units

Once you're through training, you can apply to join one of our divisions.

Special Operations Division

Responds to high-risk calls and runs tactical and specialized operations.

Traffic Services Division

Traffic enforcement, crash investigation, and keeping county roads safe.

Criminal Investigations Unit

Follows up on serious crimes and works cases through to the end.

Internal Affairs Division

Keeps the department accountable through investigations, infractions, and reviews.

Field Training Division

Runs the academy and ride-alongs that bring new deputies onto the road.

Public Relations Division

Handles announcements, media, and community events for the department.

How to join

Every deputy goes through the same path, in this order.

  1. Submit the entry application

    Tell us about yourself and your experience. Command reviews every application.

  2. Join the Discord and verify

    Verify your Roblox account in the verification channel to unlock the server.

  3. Pass the entrance exam

    A short exam on the basics before you start the academy.

  4. Complete the academy

    The Field Training Division teaches our procedures, radio codes, and standards.

  5. Finish your ride-along

    Patrol with a field training officer. Pass, and you're a Fayette County deputy.
